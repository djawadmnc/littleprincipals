Our policies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Pre-launch website

Who is responsible

LittlePrincipals is an independently operated brand based in Algeria. For privacy requests, contact littleprincipals@gmail.com. This policy distinguishes the current information website from the planned future personalization service.

Information handled now

This static website has no order forms, user accounts, upload tools, advertising pixels or analytics scripts. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Please keep enquiries to adult contact details and general product questions; do not send children's photographs, full names, birth dates, home addresses or sensitive information.

Hosting, fonts and external links

The site is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser information, to serve and protect its hosting service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's font services. We do not add tracking cookies or browser storage. Email is handled through our Gmail business inbox. Links to Facebook and Instagram take you to separate services governed by their own policies. See GitHub's privacy statement, Google's privacy policy and Google Fonts privacy information.

Purposes and retention of enquiries

We use correspondence to answer your enquiry, provide support and handle requests. We do not use enquiries for marketing without a separate opt-in. We retain correspondence only as needed to resolve the enquiry and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion; we will explain any lawful reason that prevents deletion. A fixed order-data retention schedule is not active because orders are not open.

Planned personalization service

Future purchases will be handled by parents or guardians. Necessary information may include the purchasing adult's email, the child's first name, story preferences and appearance descriptions. Optional images will only be accepted after an appropriate private submission process, guardian consent and a specific retention/deletion schedule are in place. No such upload process is active now. Do not send images by email as a substitute.

Service providers and international processing

Current enquiries use Gmail, and the planned hosting and font services are described above. These providers may process data outside Algeria. We do not currently send child personalization information to production tools or a payment provider through this website. Before collecting it, we will identify the actual providers, purposes, necessary international-processing information and retention terms. No payment provider is connected to this site.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to processing, withdraw consent where used, and raise a concern with a relevant data-protection authority. Email littleprincipals@gmail.com. We will address requests under applicable requirements and may need proportionate confirmation that the requester is entitled to the information.

Children's information

This site is directed to purchasing adults. Children should not send us personal information. If a child has submitted information without appropriate guardian involvement, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. We do not publish customer or child details in our public website source.

Updates

This policy will be updated before orders, uploads or new processing activities begin.
