Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Pre-launch website

Current status

Orders and payments are not open. This policy explains the intended support approach for future digital PDF storybooks. It is not an invitation to pay by email or an indication of payment-provider approval.

Missing, corrupted or incorrect files

If a future order is not delivered, cannot be opened, is materially different from its description or contains personalization mistakes caused by us, contact littleprincipals@gmail.com. We will investigate and offer an appropriate correction, replacement or refund under applicable consumer law. You do not have to give up a statutory refund right to request help.

Personalization mistakes

If we use different details from those you supplied, we will correct the error at no additional charge or provide another remedy required by law. If you entered incorrect details, contact us promptly; we will explain whether a correction is possible and obtain agreement before any additional work or charge. No additional fee is automatically imposed.

Cancellation and change of mind

Contact us promptly if you wish to cancel a future order. Personalized digital products may have different cancellation or withdrawal rules depending on the purchaser's location, the nature of the service and whether supply has begun. We do not apply a blanket no-refunds rule. Any legally required express consent or acknowledgment concerning digital delivery and withdrawal rights will be obtained separately before supply. These rights are not waived by reading this page.

How to request help

Email littleprincipals@gmail.com with your order reference, purchasing email and a short description of the problem. Do not send full payment-card details or a child's photograph. We will review the request, explain the available remedy and confirm any approved refund through the original payment method where possible. We do not yet publish a fixed response-time guarantee.

Consumer rights and payment-provider rules

Mandatory consumer rights, including applicable rights for defective or misdescribed digital content, take precedence. If Paddle is approved and used for future purchases, its applicable buyer terms and refund policy will also apply; additional protections will not be reduced by this policy. Paddle is not currently connected and no transactions are being accepted.
