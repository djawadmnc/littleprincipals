Meet LittlePrincipals

More than a story.
A small seed for real life.

LittlePrincipals is a children's development brand dedicated to helping parents raise kind, courageous, thoughtful, and responsible children through meaningful stories, imagination, and real-world experiences.

Our initial products will focus on children aged 3–6, using age-appropriate stories and activities that parents can enjoy alongside their children.

Beyond the Story

The adventure continues
after the last page.

A defining feature of our storytelling approach will be Beyond the Story: simple, age-appropriate real-world activities and challenges that invite children to practice what they have learned.
